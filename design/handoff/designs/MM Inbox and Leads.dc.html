Inbox and Leads, one tap away

On iPad and desktop, Inbox and Leads sit under Home and Nova in the sidebar, each with a count badge: blurple for unread, red only when something is urgent. On phone, Inbox has its own tab with Leads as a second tab inside it. Every frame works: open messages, send a drafted reply, dismiss leads, open the bell. Each size shows twice: once in the current theme, once in the other.
